Support

Something not working, or a match that never finished analysing? Email mardanov.ayrat@gmail.com and we'll get back to you within a couple of days.

It helps to include the date of the match and the club you played at, so we can find the right recording.

Good to know

Analysis takes a few hours for a full match — your reel is usually ready the same evening, not the moment you stop recording.

A match belongs to everyone who played it. Share its code and the others see the same match; deleting it removes it from your own history, and the video is deleted once everyone has removed it.
