Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Padelytics ("the app") records your padel matches and uses AI to analyze them — court calibration, player tracking, rally detection, heatmaps, and rally clips. This page explains what we collect, why, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and profile photo, from Google Sign-In. We use this only to identify you and your matches; we never see or store your Google password.
  • Match recordings — the video and audio you record of your padel matches.
  • Derived match data — player positions, rally timing, heatmap images, player thumbnails, and rally-clip videos, all generated from your recordings by our analysis pipeline.

How we use it

We use your recordings solely to run match analysis: detecting the court, tracking each player's position, finding rallies, and generating heatmaps and clips for you and your teammates to review.

While a match is analyzed, we briefly use face detection to tell players apart if they swap sides of the court, so stats stay attached to the right person. This applies to everyone captured in the recording, not just the account holder. Face data is used only during that one analysis run, is never stored afterward, is never used to identify anyone by name, and is never compared against any other recording.

Sharing

Your data is processed and stored on Google Cloud Run (compute) and Cloudflare R2 / Supabase (storage and database). We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. If you share a match code with a teammate, they can view that match's analysis and results.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data until you delete it. You can permanently delete your account and every match you've recorded — including video, heatmaps, and rally clips — at any time from the app's Profile → Delete account. If you only joined a match someone else recorded, deleting your account removes your name from that match; the recording owner's data is unaffected.

Children's privacy

Padelytics is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
