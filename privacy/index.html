Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

PadelSense ("the app") records your padel matches and analyses them: it finds the court, follows where each player moves, works out when the rallies were, and cuts a highlights reel. This page explains what it collects, why, and how to get rid of it.

Signing in

You can sign in with Google or with Apple. Either way we store an identifier for your account and your display name, so the other players in a match can see who they played with. With Google we also store the profile photo URL. With Apple we store no photo, and Apple only ever gives us your name once, at your first sign-in.

We do not store your email address at all, with either provider, and we never see your password. Signing in with Apple creates a separate account from signing in with Google — the two cannot be linked, so use the same one each time to find your matches.

Match recordings

The app records video and audio of the whole court while you play. Everyone on court is in that recording, not only the person holding the account, and everyone in frame is being recorded — please make sure the people you are playing with know that.

From each recording the analysis produces player positions, rally start and end times, heatmaps, a still of each player cut from a video frame, and short clips of the best rallies.

How players are told apart

There is no face recognition anywhere in this app. Players are distinguished by what they are wearing and by which end of the court they play from, worked out separately for each individual match. Nothing is matched against any other recording, nothing is compared between matches, and nobody is identified by name by the analysis. The picture the app shows when it asks which player is you is a crop of a whole person from a video frame, not a face.

Location

When you start a match, the app asks for your location once and uses that single reading to work out which club you are playing at, so matches are named after the venue instead of a date. The coordinates are sent to the Google Places API to look up the nearby club, and are stored alongside that match. The app does not track your location at any other time, and refusing the permission only means your matches have no club name — everything else works.

Where your data is processed

Analysis runs on Google Cloud Run, videos and clips are stored on Backblaze B2, and match records are kept in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database. Club lookups go to the Google Places API, as described above. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

If you share a match code with the other players, they can see that match — its video, its reel and its stats — and they appear in it as participants.

Deleting a match

Deleting a match removes it from your history. The other players in that match keep theirs, because it is their match too. Once every player has deleted it, the video, the chunks it was uploaded in and the highlight clips are deleted from storage for good.

What survives that is the numbers: rally times, durations and similar match statistics, kept so long-term stats stay correct. They contain no video and no images.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app. This removes your profile, and with it your name and photo everywhere they appeared. Matches you recorded that nobody else joined are deleted outright, video included. A match other players also joined stays with them — it is their recording of their game as much as yours — and you are removed from it, along with your pick of which player you were.

Children's privacy

PadelSense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
